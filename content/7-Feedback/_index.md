### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  

The work environment is quite informal and engaging; since different people are in the office each day, it’s easy to exchange ideas, track progress, and socialize with others. The workspace itself is upscale, clean, and odorless, with a beautiful view from the 26th floor. On the downside, the office can feel cramped and rather stifling due to the high number of people, and the windows are often kept closed when it’s sunny to block out the heat.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  

I haven’t relied much on support from the mentors, so I don’t have any particular feedback. From what I’ve observed on WhatsApp, most questions from the participants are answered by the mentors very quickly—even when the answers are already available in the provided materials, the mentors still take the time to respond. Their replies are consistently thorough and detailed, whether they concern office matters, policies, or group projects. That said, there have been a few instances where questions were asked but received no response.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  

AWS is a company directly related to the IT sector—specifically to my field of Information Security—making it an undeniably suitable environment for applying the skills I have acquired. Furthermore, it offers significant opportunities to expand and enhance various complementary skills.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  

During the OJT (on-the-job training) process, students must actively seek out the right components for their group projects; consequently, the opportunities for learning and skill development are virtually limitless. Students are compelled to either adapt and grow to fulfill their assigned tasks effectively, or to refine their existing skills to an exceptional level in order to successfully complete the OJT—thereby preventing a scenario where students remain stagnant, passively waiting for opportunities to fall into their laps.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  

The culture here is very positive; people are open to striking up conversations with both acquaintances and strangers. Everyone is willing to share details about their team’s projects, exchanging ideas on features to drive mutual improvement. The team spirit is also highly commendable—if some members finish their tasks ahead of the deadline while others are still working, they readily step in to help, ensuring the project reaches completion faster.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  

By providing a premium environment at the Bitexco Financial Tower, AWS offers students the chance to experience a high-end professional setting and the lifestyle of an office worker in District 1. This exposure broadens their horizons significantly and serves as a powerful motivator, encouraging them to continuously strive for self-improvement. Furthermore, the opportunities provided for students to attend various events are particularly noteworthy; these gatherings allow them to learn from the experiences and achievements shared by the hosts, while also facilitating valuable connections with businesses that could prove beneficial for their future careers.

---

### Overall thoughts

- What I appreciated most during my internship was the opportunity to work in a professional, high-end environment—one that was demanding enough to keep me from slacking off, while also allowing me to get an early taste of what life is like for a working professional.
- However, since the office space is borrowed, the amenities available to students are quite limited. Students are confined to a single designated area, and for any movement—such as going to the restroom or taking a lunch break—they must use a secondary passageway. The main entrance/exit may only be used twice: once upon arrival and once upon departure. Furthermore, students are not permitted to use the lounge or any of the provided resources (such as snacks, coffee, or the water dispenser), as these are reserved for permanent staff—a policy that is entirely understandable.
