# Summary Report: “AWS Cloud and AI Day Hanoi - Watch Party: Ho Chi Minh City”

### Event Objectives

- Create a connecting space for the community of developers, infrastructure engineers, and technology leaders in Ho Chi Minh City to livestream the year's biggest event—AWS Cloud & AI Day—taking place in Hanoi.
- Stay updated on pioneering technology trends and strategies from AWS, including Generative AI, Agentic AI, Infrastructure Modernization, and Data Foundation.
- Facilitate direct discussion and networking among AWS Builders, Student Builders, and solution experts in the Southern region.

### Speaker List

- **AWS Leadership & Technical Speakers** – Key speakers connecting live from the main event venue at the JW Marriott Hanoi.
- **AWS Solutions Architects & Community Leaders** – A team of AWS technical experts facilitating and supporting discussions at the AWS Ho Chi Minh City office.

### Key Highlights

#### Keynote: Shaping the Future with Cloud & AI

- An overview of the **Agentic AI** wave and how this technology is reshaping business process automation.
- AWS's vision for building secure, flexible, and cost-optimized cloud foundations for next-generation Generative AI models.

#### Building an AI-Ready Data Foundation (Data Foundation for Agentic AI)

- The importance of enterprise data in training and operating AI Agents.
- An introduction to core capabilities regarding the storage, governance, and integration of real-world business data to ensure accurate AI responses.
#### Real-World Applications with Amazon Bedrock & AI Agents

- Techniques for successfully deploying **AI Agents** in enterprises, moving beyond the pilot phase to full production.
- Leveraging **Amazon Bedrock** as an end-to-end platform to build, customize, and scale Generative AI applications.

#### Development Automation & Infrastructure Modernization

- Utilizing **Agent Toolkit** and AI coding agent tools to assist developers in looking up documentation and accessing AWS resources.
- Strategies for transforming legacy systems, reducing technical debt, and modernizing applications using Serverless or Container technologies.

#### Roundtable Discussion & Networking in Ho Chi Minh City

- In-person discussion in Ho Chi Minh City regarding the practical application of AWS solutions in the Southern market.
- Networking opportunities among Student Builders, developers, and IT leaders.

### Key Takeaways

#### Design Thinking & Strategy

- **Data-centric AI strategy:** Building AI foundations by cleaning and standardizing internal data.
- **Agentic Automation:** Shifting the mindset from information-retrieval chatbots to AI Agents capable of autonomous decision-making and task execution.

#### Technical Architecture

- Integrating AWS data services to enrich AI models with contextual knowledge.
- Optimizing the SDLC by incorporating AI Assistants and AI Agents into coding, testing, and system maintenance workflows.

#### Practical Application

- **Re-evaluating data strategy:** Reviewing current project data systems to prepare for AI Agent integration. - **Amazon Bedrock Experimentation:** Sign up and build Proof of Concept (PoC) applications using Generative AI.
- **Strengthening Community Connections:** Engage more deeply with the AWS User Group and Student Builders in Ho Chi Minh City to gain practical, hands-on experience.

### Event Experience

Attending the **AWS Cloud and AI Day Hanoi - Watch Party at the AWS Ho Chi Minh City office** was a fantastic experience that combined large-scale knowledge sharing with an intimate, engaging atmosphere. Key highlights included:

#### An engaging and interactive livestream viewing experience
- Despite watching the Hanoi event via a remote feed, the atmosphere at the AWS Ho Chi Minh City office remained vibrant, thanks to the excellent facilitation by the hosting team.
- On-site experts clarified and explained complex concepts from the Keynote and technical sessions during the breaks.

#### High-quality networking opportunities in the South
- The Watch Party brought together Cloud Architects, Tech Leads, and talented students from across Ho Chi Minh City.
- It provided a great platform to discuss real-world challenges that businesses in the South face when implementing Cloud and AI solutions.

#### Key Takeaways
- The Watch Party format is a highly effective way to stay updated on the latest technologies without the need for long-distance travel.
- **Agentic AI** and **Data Foundation** are two critical pillars that Cloud and AI engineers should focus on mastering in the near future.
